# Proactive features and automations

Settings, Proactive has Schedule, Reflection, Notifications and History tabs. Schedule contains Checking frequency, Proactive alerts (Notification hours and Notification limits), and Morning brief. Each group keeps its own Save action. Checking frequency configures the heartbeat’s unattended attention checks. Notification hours apply to proactive alerts; they do not suspend checks or gate approval notifications. Daily notification limit, repeat suppression and minimum urgency configure the existing wake filter. Reflection saves validated memory notes automatically; skill changes still require approval. Reflection’s Run now requires owner/Full access. The morning brief is assembled in code from held digest items and pending approvals rather than a model turn.

The wake filter decides whether output stays silent, enters a digest, or becomes a ping. It applies deduplication, active hours, urgency and budget rules. Enabled heartbeat does not mean every check sends a message. The default bot is used when heartbeat or reflection has no explicit bot selection.

Automations has schedules and supported external triggers. Cron is five-field with a minimum interval and overlap safeguards. Webhook-style events are verified before processing. GitHub polling uses outbound requests; Slack and webhook integrations have their own setup and restrictions. Triggered runs are unattended and generally isolated; they do not acquire action authority from event text.

openag_status can report whether heartbeat, reflection and the brief are enabled. It cannot prove a scheduler fired, a notification was delivered, credentials work, or a provider accepted an action. Push configuration and tokens are not exposed by that tool. If deeper diagnosis is needed, direct the owner to the relevant desk controls and authorized logs.

For notifications, openag_capabilities distinguishes disabled push, missing topic configuration and configured service. It does not expose the topic or send a test. notify_user remains an external action requiring approval; configuration does not prove provider acceptance or device delivery, and rate limits may still prevent a call.

Automation history separates execution from scheduling diagnostics. Missed means supported scheduled opportunities passed without recorded admission; Skipped explains a known busy restriction; Start failed explains a setup/admission failure; Coverage gap means past coverage is uncertain. Diagnostics show scheduled/observed times and Not started, without a turn or Work. They do not send new alerts or imply that the task was investigated. See docs/notes/automations/README.md for the authoritative behavior and limits.

Cron tracking begins at upgrade or activation. Restart records known overdue gaps without catch-up; pause/resume and schedule edits do not project the current schedule into the past. A delayed live tick may admit one current check while grouping older gaps. Polling backoff/cursors and existing wake filtering are unchanged. Repeated recovery preserves delivery evidence without sending another copy.

Standing responsibilities are separate from scheduled automations and explicit Work. In Automations, the Responsibilities tab lets the owner review exact sender/topic, permitted source resource, investigator, question and destination. Saving leaves it paused; enabling and edits require owner access and a current revision. Limited devices cannot change it. Editing pauses it for another review.

Automatic investigations use an approved source binding. The normal daemon has no live investigation source in this release: Outlook is unavailable, and synthetic acceptance does not connect an account. API/Ollama have the scoped investigation path; ACP investigations are unsupported and never fall back to unrestricted CLI tools. Ordinary ACP chat is unchanged.

Within that path, investigations can perform only curated approved reads. They receive no unrelated bot memory, vector recall, skills or conversation history. They cannot create Work, ask for action approval, delegate, send mail, create a provider draft or write memory. Proposed findings and replies are retained with source references and observation times. A suggested reply stays unsent. Missing access, records, stale evidence, execution limits and interruption do not certify business completion.

Pause, scope edits or source revocation block further reads and unpublished results. Interrupted investigations do not restart automatically. Findings open from a notice, morning brief or Investigation history. Raw investigation execution rooms are private; use those guarded views for inspection. The brief rechecks scope before showing queued result links. Stable event and result identities prevent duplicate attempts and local wake delivery; phone receipt remains unconfirmed. Previously delivered content cannot be recalled by pausing. See docs/notes/responsibilities.md for bounds and recovery details.
A failed reflection run can mean its model turn was interrupted, including by the watchdog. It does not mean a successful check found nothing to propose. See the [reflection guide](../notes/reflection.md).

Automations opens its Automations tab. New automation is in the page header. Each row has an enabled switch and an actions menu for Edit, History, Run now, webhook secret rotation and Delete. Delete and rotation require confirmation. The History tab contains recorded runs; Responsibilities has its own tab. Changing a tab does not grant action authority. See [Settings layout](../notes/settings-polish.md).

New/edit scheduled automations show the effective time zone and next scheduled time before saving. An inherited time zone comes from the daemon, not your phone/browser. Paused schedules show no scheduled run. Refresh schedule preview only reads timing; it does not save or run anything. Invalid or unavailable timing must be resolved before saving a schedule. Scheduled timing is separate from admission, execution and delivery evidence in History. Post in the room sends the agent reply to that room; Notify when needed applies the existing notification filter and may remain silent. See docs/notes/desk-part3c-ux.md.

Automations opens from Workspace navigation. History can link to one automation or one recorded run; opening a link does not run it again. Edit links reopen the saved automation, and an unknown/deleted ID shows an explicit not-found state. Proactive tabs restore through their address, including Back/Forward and refresh. Switching retained tabs preserves drafts; leaving the page still requires the existing leave choice.

Enabled, configured push also alerts you when an integration needs a response. Open the chat or Needs you → Questions to answer. Each new request sends at most one minimal notification, subject to the shared hourly cap; decisions and reloads do not send another. Provider forms and links stay in the desk. See [provider input](../notes/mcp-lifecycle.md#needs-you-server-input).
